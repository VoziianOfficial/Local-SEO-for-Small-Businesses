What gets reviewed first

The profile pieces search checks first.

Four parts of the profile most often decide whether a nearby customer trusts what they see.

Categories & Services

The primary and secondary categories, plus the listed services, tell search platforms what the business actually does.

Photos & Recent Updates

Current photos and regular updates signal an active, trustworthy business rather than an abandoned listing.

Hours & Special Hours

Incorrect hours frustrate customers directly, and they need to match what directories and the website say.

Reviews & Owner Responses

How a business responds to feedback sits right on the profile, next to the star rating everyone sees first.
