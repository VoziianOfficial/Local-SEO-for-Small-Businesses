Local metrics, translated

A metric becomes useful when it answers a business question.

No sample percentages are shown because the meaning belongs to the verified source data and the business context.

Visibility

Profile views

Shows that the listing appeared in a context where someone could inspect it. It does not prove a visit or enquiry.

Intent

Map and direction interactions

Suggests interest in place and access. Interpret alongside opening information, location type, and seasonality.

Consideration

Website visits

Moves the journey to content the business controls. Review the landing path and the next available action.

Action

Calls and form enquiries

Indicates direct contact, while call quality and enquiry fit still need business-side context.

Myths versus reality

Replace shortcuts with a more accurate picture.

Myth

A complete profile guarantees top map placement.

Reality

Completeness supports clarity, but relevance, distance, prominence, competition, search context, and platform changes also shape visibility.

Myth

More directory listings are always better.

Reality

Relevant, accurate records matter. Unsupported volume can create duplicates, management overhead, and inconsistent information.

Myth

Every service area needs a near-identical page.

Reality

Location pages should carry useful local context and genuine service information. Thin doorway pages create a poor experience.

Myth

Review growth means asking only happy customers.

Reality

Ethical review requests invite eligible customers fairly and do not filter people by expected sentiment.
