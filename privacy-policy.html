Privacy Policy

How Nearloom Local Ltd. handles personal information shared through this website and its contact routes.

Last updated 1 August 2026

On this page
  • Overview
  • Information collected
  • How information is used
  • Sharing and processors
  • Retention and security
  • Your rights
  • Contact

This policy is a practical draft for the Nearloom website. It explains the intended handling of personal information but must be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before production use, particularly for the jurisdictions and tools used by the business.

1. Overview and controller

Nearloom Local Ltd. is the intended controller of personal information submitted through this website. The temporary business address and contact email appear below and are managed through the central site configuration.

Nearloom aims to collect only the information needed to understand and respond to enquiries, deliver agreed services, manage business relationships, meet legal obligations, and protect the website from misuse.

2. Information collected

Information you provide

The contact form may collect your full name, email address, business name, optional website, city or service area, inquiry type, selected service, message, consent choice, and source page. The public form does not request a phone number.

Business service information

If a conversation progresses, Nearloom may receive business profile details, website and directory information, account-access details, service and location information, reports, and other materials needed for the agreed scope.

Technical and security information

The hosting environment may process basic request information such as IP address, user agent, request time, and server logs. The form handler may use a short-lived session identifier to reduce repeated submissions.

3. How information is used

  • To review and respond to local SEO, collaboration, advertising, or information enquiries.
  • To take steps requested before entering into a service agreement.
  • To deliver, document, and support an agreed professional service.
  • To maintain business records, invoices, scope approvals, and correspondence.
  • To protect the website, form endpoint, systems, and recipients from abuse or security incidents.
  • To meet applicable legal, accounting, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

Lawful bases

Depending on the context, processing may rely on steps requested before a contract, performance of a contract, legitimate interests in running and protecting the business, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where consent is required. The correct basis must be confirmed during legal review.

4. Sharing and processors

Nearloom does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared with hosting, email, security, professional-adviser, analytics, storage, or service-delivery providers only where reasonably needed and subject to appropriate terms.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect legal rights, to investigate misuse, or as part of a legitimate business reorganisation. If a provider stores or accesses information outside the United Kingdom, appropriate transfer safeguards should be assessed and documented.

Current implementation note

The website uses a server-side email form and a temporary configuration. Hosting, email delivery, analytics, consent tools, and final processors must be documented before launch.

5. Retention and security

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, the business relationship, dispute management, security, and legal or accounting requirements. Retention periods should be documented after the final operating systems are selected.

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so access should be limited and incidents handled through an appropriate response process.

6. Your rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data-protection authority.

Nearloom may need to verify identity before completing a request and may retain information where required or permitted by law. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

7. Contact and changes

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to Contact email. The intended postal contact is Business address.

This policy may change when the website, services, processors, law, or business practices change. The updated date at the top of the page should be revised through config/config.js.

Privacy questions

Ask before sharing details.

If something about personal information, contact routes, or data handling is unclear, send the question before submitting business context.
