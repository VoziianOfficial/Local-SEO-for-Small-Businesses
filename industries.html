Business types

Local search behaves differently for every business.

Common patterns can guide the questions, but the real scope depends on how customers search, decide, travel, call, book, or visit.

Trades & Home Services

02

03

04

Sticky business spotlight

One channel, different decisions.

A customer may need immediate reassurance about service area, timing, qualifications, and how to make contact.

Horizontal customer journey

From need to action.

01 / Need

A local situation begins.

A repair becomes urgent, a table is needed, a service is being compared, or a visit is being planned.

02 / Discovery

Search narrows the nearby options.

Relevance, distance, available information, and visible business context shape the first shortlist.

03 / Confidence

Details reduce uncertainty.

Hours, service fit, location, website content, photos, and genuine reviews help someone decide whether to continue.

04 / Action

The next step becomes easy.

A call, direction request, booking route, website visit, or form enquiry connects visibility to the business.

Fit and working conditions

Useful when change is supported.

A suitable fit

  • The business offers genuine local services, visits, or locations
  • Approved business information and profile access can be provided
  • Internal feedback and approvals can happen within a workable rhythm
  • Expectations can be tied to practical actions rather than guaranteed positions

Not a suitable fit

  • The objective depends on fake reviews or manufactured reputation
  • The location strategy requires spam-led doorway pages
  • The business expects a guaranteed map position or lead volume
  • Core information cannot be verified or used with permission
