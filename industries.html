Four common business types

Local search doesn't work the same way twice.

Category, decision speed, and travel distance all shape which local signals matter most.

Trades & Home Services

Searches often start with urgency. Service-area accuracy, phone visibility, and fast responses matter more than a polished photo gallery.

Hospitality & Food

Recent photos, current hours, and how a business responds to reviews carry unusual weight in a fast decision.

Wellness & Personal Care

Trust builds slowly. Accurate services, credentials, and a complete profile matter before a first booking happens.

Multi-Location Retail

The same business name and details need to hold up consistently across every storefront, not just the flagship location.
